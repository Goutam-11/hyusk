# Hyusk mobile visual system

Use a near-black base, pearl foreground, graphite borders, and mint-cyan only for live state. The butterfly is the sole hero mark. Prefer one composed surface over stacks of floating cards. Motion belongs in the butterfly wings, listening rings, and state transitions; controls remain quiet and predictable.

Touch targets are at least 44 px. User-facing copy uses sentence case. Status copy is brief, literal, and never claims completion before verification.
